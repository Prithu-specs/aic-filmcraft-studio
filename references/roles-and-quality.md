# Roles and quality controls

## Lean Filmcraft pass map

One person may perform all roles, but make the passes distinct.

| Pass | Core question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Producer | What is the approved scope, cost, deadline, and authority? | brief, budget reserve, gate plan |
| Writer | What changes in the viewer and in the character? | logline, beat map, screenplay |
| Director | What must the audience see and feel in each scene? | scene intention, performance/blocking notes |
| Art and continuity | What cannot drift between shots? | canon, asset registry, continuity record |
| Cinematography and lighting | How does frame, movement, light, and color tell the beat? | visual treatment, shot cards |
| Sound | What should be heard, withheld, or bridged? | spotting plan, cue list, mix priorities |
| Editor | Does every cut advance cause, emotion, or information? | edit plan, picture lock, revision list |
| QA and release | Is the final master correct, permitted, accessible, and delivered? | review evidence, delivery record |

## Required gates

- **G0: Brief approval.** Audience, scope, runtime, budget, authority, and delivery target are known.
- **G1: Story and canon lock.** Scene changes are clear; assets and their rules are versioned.
- **G2: Generation preflight.** Tool settings, inputs, cost, test purpose, and acceptance criteria are recorded.
- **G3: Clip and seam approval.** Retained footage passes creative, continuity, technical, and applicable rights checks.
- **G4: Picture and sound lock.** The editorial intent is complete before final finishing.
- **G5: Master/delivery/archive.** The output is watched and listened through, release checks pass, proof is stored, and recovery is possible.

## Failure diagnosis

| Symptom | Likely cause | Controlled response |
|---|---|---|
| character, wardrobe, or prop changes | missing or weak canon/reference scope | lock an approved reference and restate fixed details |
| action feels disconnected | no causal action or incomplete coverage | redesign the beat and generate the action/reaction/bridge |
| location or screen direction flips | geography was unspecified | add a spatial map and explicit entrances/exits |
| repeats fail the same way | prompt-only iteration on a method problem | change reference method, staging, tool route, or simplify the action |
| edit seam feels artificial | no matching end/start state or sound bridge | repair the boundary or bridge with reaction, cutaway, or sound |
| music overwhelms the scene | score was chosen before the dramatic turn | re-spot from the edit and let silence carry the moment where needed |

## Multi-person or multi-agent work

Work from pinned brief and canon versions. Contributors return candidates with assumptions, evidence, risks, and a recommended decision. Only the assigned integrator updates the active record. Independent creative and QA reviews may run in parallel; conflicting edits to the same active artifact may not.
